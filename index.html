Plant Physiology Class 11 Practical

Comparative Stomatal Density: Monocot vs Dicot Leaves

400X Magnification

Hypothesis: Dicot leaves exhibits significantly higher stomatal frequency on the abaxial (lower) surface compared to adaxial, whereas monocot is amphistomatic.

Specimens: Tradescantia (Monocot) & Hibiscus rosa-sinensis (Dicot).

Apparatus: Compound microscope, Safranin stain, Glycerine mount, Stage micrometer.

Key Finding 142 vs 38 stoma/mm² (Dicot abaxial/adaxial)
Cellular Dynamics Class 11 Practical

Osmosis & Plasmolysis in Rhoeo discolor Leaves

10% KNO3 Solution

Hypothesis: Hypertonic salt solution induces protoplast shrinkage away from the cellulosic plant cell wall via exosmosis.

Specimens: Lower epidermis of Rhoeo discolor (pigmented anthocyanin cell sap).

Procedure: Sequential mounting with pure water vs 10% KNO3; monitored protoplast detachment over 15 min.

Deplasmolysis 100% reversible in hypotonic bath
